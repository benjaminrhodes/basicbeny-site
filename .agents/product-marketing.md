# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-08-24*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Basic Beny sells practical creative tools and personalized sessions that help music makers move projects forward.
**What it does:** Basic Beny provides one-on-one track feedback, production guidance, and project support, with digital creative tools planned as a scalable product line.
**Product category:** Music production education, feedback, and Ableton tools.
**Product type:** Service business plus digital products.
**Business model:** Paid sessions and digital-product sales through basicbeny.com.

## Target Audience
**Primary audience:** Emerging and intermediate electronic music producers, including people who want to become active creators.
**Initial wedge:** Ableton users and electronic music producers, with drum and bass as the strongest genre specialty.
**Primary use case:** Get unstuck, improve a track, solve a production problem, and finish more ideas.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Hear a project clearly through an experienced second set of ears.
- Solve arrangement, sound-selection, workflow, or mix bottlenecks.
- Learn techniques in the context of a real project rather than generic lessons.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Producers accumulate promising ideas but lose momentum when they cannot identify the next useful decision.
**Why alternatives fall short:** Generic tutorials lack project context; informal feedback is often vague, performative, or gatekept.
**Emotional tension:** Frustration, second-guessing, unfinished work, and anxiety about whether a track is good enough.

## Differentiation
- Practical feedback grounded in the customer’s actual project.
- Producer-to-producer guidance rather than generic instruction.
- Welcoming and judgment-free without lowering the quality bar.
- Technical depth when useful, always tied to a creative outcome.

## Customer Language
**Words to use:** practical, finish, clear next move, stuck, track, project, hands-on, judgment-free.
**Words to avoid:** unlock your potential, elevate, revolutionary, world-class, guaranteed results.

## Brand Voice
**Tone:** Direct, welcoming, technically capable, and anti-gatekeeping.
**Style:** Clear over clever; practical over motivational; confident without hype.
**Personality:** Futuristic, creative, human, and community-minded.

## Proof Points
Only publish verifiable work samples, testimonials, credits, or metrics with permission. Do not invent social proof.

## Goals
**Primary business goal:** Establish consistent session and digital-product revenue, with $1,000/month as the first milestone.
**Primary conversion action:** Book a one-on-one session.
**Secondary conversion action:** Buy or join the waitlist for a named digital product once available.
