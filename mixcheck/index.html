// MixCheck

Hear the low end before you call it done.

Upload a stereo mix and pick a lane. You get a short read, in plain language, on the things bass music actually gets wrong: loudness against streaming targets, true peak, whether the sub holds up in mono, whether the kick and the sub are stepping on each other, and how the balance sits against a generic profile for that lane.

The file stays in this tab. MixCheck reads it here. Nothing is uploaded. Nothing comes back mastered. There is no file to download.

MixCheck is free. It is not its own product. It is a straight look at the meters, and then one sensible session if you want Beny on the record with you.

There is no hit score. A meter cannot hear groove, arrangement, or whether you picked the right sound. When the read is past its ears, it says so.

// What the meters never hear

Groove and swing. Whether the drum pattern breathes. Arrangement, and whether the drop earns the build. Sound choice: the Reese, the kick, the hat. Emotion. Taste.

Those are musical calls. They are the reason to sit with another producer. They are not a reason to trust a number.
