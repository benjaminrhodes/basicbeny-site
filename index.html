// About Basic Beny

Built by a producer who knows what stuck feels like.

I’m Basic Beny, a drum and bass producer based in Dallas, Texas. I build tools and offer sessions around the same problem I face in my own work: turning a promising idea into something finished.

My approach is practical, direct, and judgment-free. We can get technical when the work calls for it, but the goal is never to bury you in theory. It’s to help you hear the project clearly and make the next useful decision.

Every tool and session comes from real production experience—not a generic formula for how your music is supposed to sound.

// Before you book

Common questions.

Do I need a finished track?

No. Rough ideas and unfinished projects are welcome. The clearer you are about where you feel stuck, the more focused we can make the session.

Do I need to use Ableton Live?

Ableton is where I can offer the most hands-on help. Track feedback can still be useful regardless of which DAW you use.

Is this only for drum and bass?

No. Drum and bass is my specialty, but the core work—arrangement, sound selection, workflow, mix decisions, and finishing—applies across electronic music.

Which session should I choose?

Choose Track Feedback for a focused second opinion. Choose Production Deep Dive when you want to open the project and work through a problem together. Use Project Intensive for multiple tracks or a larger creative plan.
